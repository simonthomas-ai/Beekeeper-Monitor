## Permission evidence checklist

- Original post URL and image URL
- Original creator's identity or handle and their confirmation of rights
- Date and channel of permission
- Exact license and version, or a clear written grant covering **all**
  intended uses (including AI training, redistribution and public datasets)
- Attribution name and any restrictions
- Screenshot/export of the permission message retained privately
- Independent expert species review, provenance, deduplication and annotation

Do not publish private contact details or permission screenshots in the public
repository. Do not assume social-media terms or CC BY-NC are compatible with
all intended redistribution and model uses. For ambiguous grants, seek
clarification before import.
